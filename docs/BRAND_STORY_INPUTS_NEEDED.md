# Brand Story — Inputs Needed

`/about` is built and structured, but its content is limited to what the brand
already says publicly. No founding date, founder biography, city of origin or
production story appears anywhere, because none of it could be verified.

Rather than invent a founder narrative, the page holds the structure open. Each
question below maps to a section that is ready to receive an answer.

## Origin

1. When did INFNITY start? Month and year.
2. Where is it based? The site is a `.ca` domain — which city, and does that
   city matter to the brand's identity?
3. Who founded it? Do they want to be named and visible, or stay behind the label?
4. What prompted starting it? A gap in the market, a personal need, a scene?

## The name

5. Is the missing `i` in **INFNITY** deliberate? The rebuild currently says it
   is, and reads it as "still becoming". If that is wrong, it needs replacing —
   it is the one interpretive claim on the page.
6. Which spelling is canonical going forward: INFNITY or Infinity? Two names in
   circulation splits search authority and confuses customers.
7. Is there a story behind "Be the Statement"?

## Design philosophy

8. What decides whether a piece gets made? What would never get made?
9. Which garment best represents the brand, and why that one?
10. Is there a signature construction detail — a cut, a finish, a weight?

## Product facts

These block product pages, not just the story. Every one is currently `null` and
renders as an omitted section.

11. Fabric composition per product or per category.
12. Fabric weights (gsm) where "heavyweight" is claimed. The word is currently
    used on the homepage as brand voice; if it is a product claim it needs a
    number behind it.
13. Fit descriptions: oversized, true to size, boxy?
14. Care instructions.
15. Where garments are made.
16. Garment measurements per size. The size guide renders these as "pending"
    until supplied — nothing has been estimated.

## Community

17. What is The Troop, in the brand's own words? Is it a name for customers, a
    programme, or something more specific?
18. Are there customers whose fits the brand wants featured, and is there
    permission on record to publish their images and handles?
19. What does "For the FLY only" mean to the brand? It appears in the site's
    public description but is not currently used in the rebuild.

## The Vault

20. Which collections or drops have there been? Names and dates.
21. Which pieces are genuinely archived — sold out and not returning?
22. Are there stories behind past drops worth telling?

The Vault currently derives its contents from availability data alone. No
season, drop label or release date is displayed anywhere unless it came from
verified source data — none has, so none is shown.

## Visual identity

23. Logo files, in vector if possible.
24. Is there a brand typeface? The rebuild uses Anton and Archivo as
    complements, not reproductions.
25. Is black the only brand colour, or is there an accent?
26. Which existing campaign imagery should lead the homepage hero?

## Priority

If only a few can be answered now, these unblock the most:

1. **Q11–Q16** — product facts. They are the largest visible gap, sitting on
   every product page.
2. **Q6** — the canonical spelling. It affects every page's metadata.
3. **Q23/Q26** — logo files and a hero image. They turn the homepage from
   strong typography into the flagship it is designed to be.
