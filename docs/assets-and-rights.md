# Assets and Rights

Every image the storefront serves came from the owner's own store,
`infinityclo.ca`, during the import on 2026-11-16. Nothing is generated,
retouched, upscaled or sourced from stock, and no image from the inspiration
site is used anywhere.

### Processing applied

- Product and brand images were requested from Shopify's CDN at a maximum width
  of 1600 px. No local editing.
- 19 community images arrived as multi-megabyte PNG screenshots and were
  re-encoded to JPEG (quality 86) to keep the repository and page weight sane.
  Pixels, crop and content are unchanged.
- The header emblem is trimmed of its transparent margin for layout.

## Community wall — permission status

The 34 wall images are **screenshots of customers' Instagram posts and stories**,
with the poster's handle and Instagram interface visible in the frame. They were
published by the brand on its own site under the invitation "Wanna be added to
the wall? Post and tag us @infnity.clo to be here".

That is evidence of an informal permission model (tag us to be featured), not a
recorded consent per person. So:

- They are shown, because the brand already publishes them and the wall is a
  named brand strength.
- Each is individually hideable in `/admin → Community` and none carries a
  written credit added by us (the handle visible inside a screenshot is part of
  the original image).
- **Owner action:** confirm you are comfortable carrying all 34 over, and hide
  any from people who have since asked to come down or who you cannot vouch for.
- New submissions through `/community` record explicit consent text, the
  submitter's email and a timestamp, and stay hidden until approved.

Recommended follow-up: replace screenshots with the original photos (requested
from each customer), which would also remove the Instagram interface from the
frame.

## Third-party marks inside product graphics

Several tee graphics depict or name third parties ("LeCaptain America",
"Lil Yachty", "CLB", "Lucki Star"). These are the owner's existing products and
are displayed as the store has them. Whether they are licensed is the owner's
question to answer; it is flagged here because a higher-profile storefront
raises the visibility of it.

## What must not be used

- Anything from `unashamed.site`: photographs, logo, scripture, product copy.

- Instagram content that is not the brand's own post, without the poster's consent.
