stock, AI-generated, or taken from the design reference (unashamed.site). The
per-photo manifest is `docs/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md` (generated by
`npm run import:catalog`).

## What was deliberately not done

- No Instagram scraping (the profile requires login); handles and direction were taken from the owner-supplied screenshot only.
- No upscaling of Instagram thumbnails into heroes.
- No generated or retouched faces; crops only.
- Nothing from unashamed.site beyond layout ideas.

7. **Next drop** — name, photos, colourways, sizes, price, stock and confirmed release time, when ready (enter in Admin → Drops and Products).
